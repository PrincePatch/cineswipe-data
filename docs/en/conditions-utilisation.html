Purpose

CinéSwipe is a free film-discovery app: it shows one film at a time, you skip it or you keep it, it offers you another.

The app can be used entirely without an account. An account is optional: it only serves the community side — publishing lists, commenting, following other members, exchanging messages.

Using the app means accepting these terms. If you do not accept any point of them, do not use the app. There is no subscription to cancel; if you created an account, deleting it is immediate and takes one gesture from the settings.

Your data

Without an account, everything stays with you: the films you keep and your filters are stored on your device. There is no backup on the publisher side — clearing the app data, changing device or uninstalling it is enough to lose them permanently. You can export them from the "Privacy and data" page: that is the only backup possible, and it is yours.

With an account, your email address, your profile and what you publish are stored in a database. Those are backed up by the host and follow you from one device to another.

The films you keep and your filters stay local in both cases: they are never synchronised, even with an account.

The detail is in the privacy policy.
